How these numbers are calculated

  • Collected revenue: sum of gross_amount_inr − refund_inr where payment_status = Paid and paid_at is in the period. Null refund is treated as unknown and that row is excluded from net collected rather than forced to zero.
  • Target: sum of monthly target_amount_inr for months that overlap the selected period, filtered by revenue type.
  • Variance: collected revenue minus target. Positive = above target, negative = below target.
  • Achievement %: collected ÷ target. Not annualised.
  • Average journey time: mean days from "New lead" stage event to "Enrolled" stage event, counting only leads that reached Enrolled. Calculated from lead_stage_events timestamps.
  • Workshop seats: for paid workshop rows, seats = gross ÷ ₹3,999 when that divides evenly. Pending workshop payments are not counted as seats or revenue.
  • Trials vs full programmes: payment revenue_type and enrollment program_type. These can differ; the overview cards use payments for money and enrollments for headcount.
  • Funnel conversion: distinct leads created in-period who reached stage N ÷ distinct leads created in-period who reached stage N−1. Paid conversion uses the Enrolled stage, not a separate payments join, so payment failures are visible as data-quality notes instead of silent funnel inflation.
  • CTR: clicks ÷ impressions when impressions > 0, else unavailable.
  • Cost per qualified lead: spend ÷ campaign qualified_leads when that count > 0, else unavailable. This uses ads-platform qualified counts, not the CRM is_qualified flag.
  • Outstanding invoices: max(invoice_amount − paid_amount, 0) where invoice amount > paid amount. Invoice paid amounts are not assumed to equal the payments table; mismatches are listed as reconciliation items.
